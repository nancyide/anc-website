Subject Categories in the ANC New York Times newswire Sub-corpus

The following categories appear in the headers of the New York Times data. Each header gives the following information in the <profileDesc> sub-element of the <header> element, for example:

<header>
...
   <profileDesc>
      <textClass>
         <domain>Periodicals</domain>
         <subdomain>National Newspaper</subdomain>
         <subject>Finance</subject>
         <audience>Adult</audience>
         <medium>Periodical</medium>
      </textClass>
   </profileDesc>
