Anc-discuss Mailing List

NOTE: The anc-discuss mailing list is currently inactive.

For questions, comments, and discussion concerning the use of the ANC and related data, you may join the anc-discuss mailing list.

To subscribe to the list send an email message to anc-discuss-request [at] anc.org with the word “subscribe” (no quotes) in the subject line. You will be sent a confirmation message that you will need to reply to. Just “hit reply” and send; you don’t even have reply from the same email account. Once subscribed, you can send messages to the list at anc-discuss [at] anc.org.
