Contribute Annotations

Annotations contributed to the ANC and/or the OANC will be rendered into LAF/GrAF stand-off format, with links into to the original data. The stand-off documents will be freely downloadable from the ANC website.

Derived data, such as word lists or databases, will also be freely distributed on this site.

To contribute annotations or other data derived from the ANC, contact anc-contrib@anc.org.
