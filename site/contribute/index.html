contribute

The ANC is a community collaborative project that relies on its users to contribute resources to the effort. The ANC project has not enjoyed the funding and contribution of language data that projects such as the BNC relied on for their completion. Instead, we depend for our success on contributions of individuals and project teams to provide us with additional data, annotations, and derived data in order to grow the OANC and MASC.
