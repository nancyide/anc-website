The ANC Consortium

ANC Consortium members include publishers, software companies, and academic members. Consortium members have exclusive access throughout the development period and for five years after the first installment of the corpus becomes available. Members who join after December 31, 2001 are given the same rights to early access, but pay an increased membership fee of $40,000, payable in two annual payments of $20,000 each.

To join or inquire about the ANC Consortium, contact the Project Manager, Randi Reppen.
