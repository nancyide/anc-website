Before uploading your document, please supply the following information. Note that if no attribution is desired, the Last Name field can be filled with "anonymous". If you are uploading several texts one by one, the information from the previous upload will be filled in. If you are contributing multiple documents of the same type (for example, several college essays, a group of texts from a blog, etc.) you can upload them together in a folder that has been put in a compressed format such as zip, gz, tar, etc. In this case, fill in the Title field with a descriptive title (e.g., "Various academic essays", "Blog data from ...").

Note: Only click the Submit button once. Your upload will begin as soon as you click the button but the next page will not display until the upload is complete. The length of time required for the upload to complete will depend on the size of the document and the speed of your internet connection. It could be as long as several minutes. Please be patient.

Note: We ask that all contributors permit us to distribute the entire document. Authors also have the option of requesting that we only include a portion of their text.

If you have any comments, questions, or additional information to provide, please contact us.
